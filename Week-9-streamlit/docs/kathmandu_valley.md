# Kathmandu Valley — Travel Guide

## Overview
Kathmandu Valley sits at roughly 1,400 meters elevation and is home to
three ancient royal cities: Kathmandu, Patan (Lalitpur), and Bhaktapur.
The valley holds seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites within a compact
area, making it one of the densest concentrations of heritage sites
anywhere in the world.

## Best Time to Visit
- **October–November**: Peak season. Clear skies, mountain views, and
  festivals like Dashain and Tihar.
- **March–April**: Second best window. Warm days, rhododendrons bloom
  in surrounding hills.
- **June–August**: Monsoon season. Heavy rain, landslide risk on
  mountain roads, but fewer crowds and lush green valleys.
- **December–February**: Cold, especially at night (can drop to 1–2°C
  in Kathmandu), but dry and sunny during the day.

## Key Sites
- **Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple)**: Hilltop stupa overlooking the
  valley, one of the oldest religious sites in Nepal, dating back over
  1,500 years.
- **Pashupatinath Temple**: Major Hindu temple complex on the Bagmati
  River, one of the holiest Shiva temples in South Asia. Non-Hindus
  cannot enter the main temple but can view cremation ghats from across
  the river.
- **Boudhanath Stupa**: One of the largest stupas in the world, center
  of Kathmandu's Tibetan Buddhist community.
- **Durbar Square (Kathmandu, Patan, Bhaktapur)**: Former royal
  palace complexes, each with distinct architecture. Bhaktapur's
  square suffered heavy damage in the 2015 earthquake but has been
  substantially restored.

## Getting Around
Ride-sharing apps (Pathao, InDrive) work well within the valley.
Traffic is heavy during 8–10 AM and 5–7 PM. Tourist buses connect
Kathmandu to Pokhara (6-7 hours) and Chitwan (5-6 hours).

## Practical Notes
- Tourist visas can be obtained on arrival at Tribhuvan International
  Airport for most nationalities (15/30/90-day options).
- Altitude sickness is not a concern in the valley itself (1,400m) but
  becomes relevant above 2,500m on trekking routes.
- Air quality is often poor from November to March due to dust and
  vehicle emissions; a mask is recommended for sensitive travelers.
