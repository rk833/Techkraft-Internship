# Pokhara — Travel Guide

## Overview
Pokhara sits at about 820m elevation beside Phewa Lake, roughly 200km
west of Kathmandu (25-minute flight, or 6-7 hours by road/tourist bus).
It's the main gateway for Annapurna region treks and Nepal's adventure
sports hub.

## Things to Do
- **Phewa Lake boating**: Rowboats and small paddleboats available
  hourly near Lakeside; sunrise/sunset paddles to Tal Barahi Temple
  (an island shrine) are popular.
- **Paragliding**: Launches from Sarangkot (1,600m) with landing near
  Phewa Lake. Tandem flights run roughly 25-35 minutes, weather
  permitting, typically best in the morning before winds pick up.
- **Sarangkot sunrise viewpoint**: Panoramic views of the Annapurna and
  Dhaulagiri ranges; a short taxi or ~2 hour hike from Lakeside.
- **World Peace Pagoda**: Hilltop stupa reachable by boat across Phewa
  Lake followed by a ~45 minute uphill walk, or by road.
- **Bungee jumping / canyon swing**: Available at The Bridge (near
  Kushma, ~2 hours from Pokhara), one of the higher bungee points in
  Asia.

## Trekking Gateway
Pokhara is the starting point for most Annapurna region treks:
- Annapurna Base Camp (ABC): 7-10 days
- Annapurna Circuit: 12-18 days depending on route/transport used
- Poon Hill (Ghorepani): 4-5 days, popular short trek with sunrise
  mountain views, suitable for less experienced trekkers

## Best Time to Visit
Same broad pattern as the rest of Nepal: October-November and
March-April are best for clear mountain views. Monsoon (June-August)
clouds over the Annapurna views most days but keeps the lake and
surrounding hills green.

## Practical Notes
- Lakeside is the main tourist accommodation strip, walkable, dense
  with guesthouses, restaurants, and trekking gear shops (both rental
  and cheap imitation gear).
- ATMs are widely available in Lakeside; card payment acceptance is
  more common here than in most of Nepal outside Kathmandu.
- Pokhara's domestic airport also serves as a hub for Mustang region
  flights (Jomsom).
