I went in expecting very little and came out genuinely impressed. The first
half hour drags, and there is a subplot about the brother that goes nowhere at
all, but once the story settles it becomes something quite moving. The lead
performance carries it. The score is intrusive in places, loud enough that I
missed a couple of lines of dialogue, and the ending wraps up faster than it
has earned. Still, I would watch it again, and I have already recommended it
to two people.
